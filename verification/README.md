# Workbooks and Worksheet Guide

Download a local workbook copy and open it in Excel to inspect the records and calculations.

## Qualified Records

[Qualified Records workbook](qualified-records.xlsx)

- **S0 Qualified Executions worksheet/tab:** one row per qualifying execution; find Cell ID OPVAL-P4A-S0-T for the ten admitted P4A outputs.
- **S0 Qualified Verification worksheet/tab:** property-level ensemble rows and population summaries. Table conditions appear in OPVAL, separate PROSP, LOOCV0, and LOOCV1 order; No Table detail and summaries follow below. P4A's Mean F is the ten-run ensemble prediction; SD(F), CV(F), and APE describe its precision and outcome comparison.

## Registry Records

[Registry Records workbook](registry-records.xlsx)

- **S0 Registry Executions worksheet/tab:** execution identity, QC disposition, and replacement history. P4A retains excluded E005 and replacement E011.
- **S0 Registry Verification worksheet/tab:** aggregate qualifying and excluded attempt counts, including the OPVAL Table/No Table comparison.
